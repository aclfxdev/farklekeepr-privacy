Farkle Keepr

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

Farkle Keepr is a scorekeeping app for the dice game Farkle. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it's handled. We built Farkle Keepr to keep score, not to track you — there's no advertising in the app, and we don't sell or share your data with data brokers or ad networks.

Information We Collect

To let you sign into your account and keep your game history synced across devices, we collect:

  • Email address and/or phone number — used to create and sign you into your account, and to verify it's really you.
  • Game data — scores, game history, player names, and settings you enter while using the app.

We don't ask for or collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or browsing activity outside the app.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information above only to run the app itself:

  • Authenticating you and keeping your account secure.
  • Syncing your scores and game history across your devices.
  • Letting you create or join a shared game with a 4-digit code.
  • Sending account-related emails, like verifying your email address.

We do not use your email address or phone number for advertising, and we do not combine it with data from other companies' apps or websites.

Third-Party Services We Use

We rely on a small number of infrastructure providers to run the app. They process your data only to provide their service to us — not for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

Supabase — hosts our database and handles account authentication. Your email/phone number and game data are stored securely with Supabase on our behalf.

Resend — delivers our transactional emails, such as email verification messages, when you sign up or need to confirm your account.

Apple (App Store / In-App Purchase) — if you purchase the Premium Edition Upgrade, that one-time purchase is processed entirely through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We never see or store your payment card details — Apple handles that directly.

In-App Purchases

Farkle Keepr offers an optional, one-time Premium Edition Upgrade. It's a single purchase — not a subscription — and unlocks features like custom game codes, cross-device shared history, and additional themes and icons. Purchase and payment information is handled entirely by Apple; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed.

Data Retention & Your Choices

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, contact us using the information below and we'll take care of it.

Children's Privacy

Farkle Keepr is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Security

We rely on industry-standard security practices provided by our infrastructure partners (including encryption in transit) to help protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or want to request that your data be deleted? Reach out at privacy@farklekeepr.com.
